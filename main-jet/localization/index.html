Inspect the calculation

For a mapped pixel: 3D point = w₀ × vertex₀ + w₁ × vertex₁ + w₂ × vertex₂. The weights and triangle come from the fixed correspondence record. Target A refers to missing material on the intact reference; target B refers to the remaining damaged surface.

How this connects to the current work

Location first. Shape change next.

This experiment asked “Where is the suspected damage on a known blade?” The later model asks “How did the blade surface change?” and predicts vertex movements from paired images and a healthy mesh. Both have implemented examples; reliable performance remains unfinished.
