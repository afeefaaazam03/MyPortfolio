Evidence and display changes

The viewers replay saved research outputs. This package adds a research webpage, navigation and interactive display controls, converts local source references to portable identifiers, and includes the previously refreshed presentation PDF. The homepage figures are byte-identical source images or direct WebGL exports of the saved fields; changing the camera does not change the geometry. It does not alter scientific mesh arrays, damage masks, numerical results, or model weights. No training or inference ran during packaging.

The real viewer’s display triangles join existing neighbouring estimated points; they are not independently measured surface geometry. The two 3D branches have different inputs and evidential limits, which remain visible.
