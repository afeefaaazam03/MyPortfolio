The repository normalized the source surface, added an artificial collar/closure,
and constructed controlled synthetic indentations with outward compensating
rims. These are alterations to the public sample, not authenticated physical blade
damage. This presentation copies accepted synthetic rest/deformed renders and
known mesh coordinates, and displays raw learned displacement derivatives with reference-based
vertex error colors. No new synthetic observation was rendered for this viewer. Safran/PLAID do not endorse the presentation.

Rotor37-derived images, mesh coordinate derivatives and presentation figures in
this folder are provided under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Retain attribution, identify
changes and follow share-alike terms when redistributing derivatives. No private
industrial photograph, learned checkpoint or confidential CAD is included.
